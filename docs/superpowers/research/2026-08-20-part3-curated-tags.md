**Coverage & caveats (Pedal Steel).** Built from a full-text sweep of Jerrybase per-song footnotes ("pedal steel" / "steel"; ~246 hits reviewed) followed by per-event verification of act header, musician credits, and setlist footnotes — a method that cleanly separates a GD set from an NRPS set the same night because Jerrybase files them as separate events. Deliberately excluded: every NRPS-set date (the whole 1970–71 GD/NRPS double-bill era, e.g. 1970-05-01, 1970-10-24, 1970-11-07, 1971-04-15), all studio/session steel work (*Workingman's Dead*, *American Beauty*, "Teach Your Children", Crosby's "Laughing" 1970-11-04, CSN, Stills, Rowan Bros, Lamb, "Pride of Cucamonga" 1974), and the 1970-06-30 Festival Express train jams (steel there is partly Buddy Cage; not a GD set). **Notable gap: 1971 has zero documented GD-set pedal steel** — every 1971 steel reference found resolves to NRPS. Two 1970 Fillmore West nights (02-06, 02-08) had the steel onstage but unplayed per Michael Parrish, so no rows. The songs named in the brief ("Dire Wolf", "Candyman", "High Time", "Brokedown Palace", "Friend of the Devil", "Cumberland Blues") carry Garcia steel on the *studio* albums; live GD steel is overwhelmingly country covers plus 1972's "Looks Like Rain". Could not verify: the 1969-04-26 and 1969-09-27 claims (single secondary source, no tape); whether Jerrybase's musician-credit field flags shows its footnotes miss (an exhaustive 1969–72 credit crawl hit rate limiting, though spot checks matched); and the exact Europe '72 count — one secondary source says two nights, Jerrybase documents three. dead.net blocks automated fetches, so its pedal-steel feature and Deadcast episodes could not be mined. **Venue discrepancy:** the pedal-steel pass labeled 1987-07-10 as Giants Stadium and 1987-07-12 as JFK Stadium; the historic-event and guest passes both put JFK Philadelphia on 07-10 and Giants Stadium on 07-12. The dates are stable, the venue labels need one more check. Finally, the four 1987 rows are Dylan-set songs inside GD-headlined shows — tag accordingly if "pedal steel during the Dead's own set" is meant strictly; 1987-07-19 and 1987-07-26 show no steel credit.

**Coverage & caveats (Acoustic Set).** Three clusters plus a one-off: two accidental 1969 warm-ups; the sustained 1970 *Workingman's/American Beauty* era (Feb–Nov 1970, ~60 dates — mid-show acoustic segments Feb–Apr, separate opening acoustic sets from 1970-05-01 on); the fall 1980 acoustic/electric/electric tour (25 shows: 15 Warfield, 2 Saenger, 8 Radio City, all confirmed; dark nights 9/28, 10/1, 10/5, 10/8, 10/12, 10/20–21, 10/24, 10/28); and 1981-10-16 Melkweg. Several premises in the brief did not hold up. **Ludlow Garage**: no GD show is documented there in any source (likely confused with the Allman Brothers' April 1970 run). **Winterland**: 1970-10-04/05 and 1970-12-23/31 were all-electric, so the only candidate is the unverified 1970-04-15 benefit. **Kresge Plaza / MIT 1970-05-06**: the free strike concert was a single 90-minute *electric* set; the acoustic MIT set was the next night at DuPont Gym. **Festival Express**: only the 1970-07-04 Calgary afternoon set was acoustic — not Toronto (6/27–28) or Winnipeg (7/1). **1971**: no acoustic sets; the format was dropped after Port Chester in Nov 1970. **1981-10-15 Melkweg**: the first Amsterdam night was all-electric. Excluded as not-the-band: the 1981-10-11 Weir/Garcia acoustic duo appearance and the many 1980s Weir/Wasserman and Garcia-acoustic-band openers. Low-confidence rows (1970-02-11, 04-10, 04-15, 05-08, 05-09, 08-05, 11-05) rest on a single secondary source or a missing/partial tape. The late-July/August 1970 club shows (Matrix, Lion's Share, Thee Club) are documented as acoustic but have no circulating tapes and inconsistent dating between sources.

**Coverage & caveats (Festival).** Several brief candidates were checked and **excluded because the Dead did not play them**: **Fantasy Fair & Magic Mountain (1967-06-10/11)** — the Dead appear on some retrospective lineup lists but were in New York that week and the date is absent from the deadessays 1967 show list, so this is best treated as a persistent myth; **Atlanta International Pop Festival 1969 and 1970** — the Dead played the promoters' free Piedmont Park thank-you shows instead (1969-07-07, 1970-07-07), which were not festivals; **Texas International Pop Festival 1969** (not on the bill); **Wild West Festival 1969** (Dead scheduled 8/22 but the whole festival was cancelled 8/13); **Kickapoo Creek 1970-05-30** (Dead not in the lineup; no Dead show listed for that date at all); **Sky River II 1970** and **Powder Ridge 1970** (cancelled / not played); **New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival** (never the Grateful Dead — only Dead & Company in 2023); **Telluride Town Park 1987-08-15/16** (Dead-only headline shows tied to the Harmonic Convergence, not multi-act); and **Lille 1972-05-13**, **Rotterdam 1972-05-10**, **Bremen 1972-04-21** — all standalone Europe '72 dates (Lille a free rescheduled make-good, Bremen a TV studio taping), not festivals. Red Rocks, Rainbow Theatre, Roosevelt Stadium, Hollywood Sportatorium, Laguna Seca and Frost are ordinary venue runs and correctly out. Weakest rows: the two **Trips Festival** dates — the Dead certainly played the festival, but night-by-night attribution rests on the surviving Saturday program and Sunday handbill rather than a review or tape, and there is no evidence they played Friday 1966-01-21. **1973-07-27** and **1975-03-23** are judgment calls (a soundcheck at a festival site; a multi-act benefit not branded as a festival) and are flagged medium accordingly. No further genuine multi-act festival appearance was found after 1982 through 1995. Note that **1969-12-06 is a non-performance row** (as in section 3) — do not treat it as a playable show.
